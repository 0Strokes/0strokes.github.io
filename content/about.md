I spent years breaking into things for a living. Then I switched sides and started designing secure doors, which turned out to be harder, because a secure door still has to open for the right people. I still break things, though. You outgrow most of what you loved as a kid. Not this.

These days I'm a Principal Security Engineer. In practice that means I read the scripts nobody reads and get invited to more meetings than I'd like.

This blog is where the notes end up. I write them at 2am because my memory has the retention policy of `/tmp`: everything gets wiped on the next reboot.
